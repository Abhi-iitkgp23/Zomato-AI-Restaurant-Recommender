## Brand & Style

This design system targets urban Gen-Z food lovers, late-night crawlers, and tech-forward diners across Bangalore's bustling culinary map. The aesthetic merges hyper-modern Glassmorphism with an Electric Dark interface—conveying spontaneity, tastemaker confidence, and algorithmic precision.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Atmosphere**: Near-black void punctured by vivid, kinetic chromatic fluid blooms (hot pink, electric violet, neon lime, amber).
- **Surface Quality**: Smoked, frosted-glass layering over liquid ambient gradients, delivering depth without visual clutter.
- **Attitude**: Hyper-confident, punchy, curated, and frictionless. Micro-interactions should feel tactile, snappy, and tactilely responsive.

## Colors

The chromatic palette pairs deep-space obsidian neutrals with hyper-saturated, electric accents designed to replicate midnight Bangalore street neon and screen glare.

### Color Roles & Ratios
- **Base Canvas (`#0B0B0F`)**: Dominates 75% of viewports. Establishes rich OLED depth, preventing ocular fatigue.
- **Glass Tint (`rgba(22, 22, 29, 0.75)`)**: The structural container tier for cards, bottom sheets, and navigational headers.
- **Primary Accent (`#FF3D7F`)**: Hot pink driving critical conversions, hero CTAs, live match indicators, and craving triggers.
- **Secondary Accent (`#7C5CFF`)**: Electric violet dedicated to AI discovery markers, recommendations, and conversational system states.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#C6FF3D`)**: Neon lime reserved for positive ratings, dietary tags (Pure Veg/Vegan), open statuses, and high-energy highlights.
- **Quaternary Accent (`#FFB800`)**: Warm amber for bookmarking, spice indices, late-night badges, and price metrics.
- **Borders & Rules (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)`)**: Fine 1px hairline edges separating frosted glass panels from background glow blobs.
- **Text & Contrast Hierarchy**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) for bold titles and headers (meeting WCAG AAA against `#0B0B0F`), high-contrast muted cool grey (`#B0B0C4`) for body copy (guaranteeing > 4.5:1 WCAG AA), and faint metadata silver (`#76768E`).

## Typography

The pairing marries the geometric energy of Space Grotesk for chunky, high-impact titles with the neutral legibility of Inter for continuous reading and complex metadata sets.

- **Display & Headlines**: Space Grotesk set with tight, punchy letter-spacing (-0.02em to -0.03em) and tight line heights creates an editorial, poster-style punch reminiscent of contemporary street culture.
- **Body & Data**: Inter provides uncompromised legibility across tiny ratings, opening hours, AI prompt bubbles, and ingredients lists.
- **Casing Rules**: Use uppercase strictly for micro-tags (`label-sm`) and filter metadata with widened letter-spacing (`0.05em`) to ensure legibility on dark glass.

## Layout & Spacing

A responsive fluid grid architecture organized by modular card units, tailored for one-handed mobile use and expansive desktop curation boards.

### Grid Layouts
- **Mobile (up to 639px)**: 4 columns, `margin`: `1rem` (16px), `gutter`: `1rem` (16px). Bottom sheets, horizontally scrolling carousels, and edge-to-edge media cards.
- **Tablet (640px to 1023px)**: 8 columns, `margin`: `2rem` (32px), `gutter`: `1.5rem` (24px). Dual-column AI curation feeds.
- **Desktop (1024px and up)**: 12 columns, max-width `1280px` centered, `margin`: `3rem` (48px), `gutter`: `2rem` (32px). Persistent map view split with recommendation cards.

### Spacing Principles
- Component internals rely on `space-sm` (8px) and `space-md` (16px).
- Vertical stack rhythm between distinct culinary cards adheres to `space-lg` (24px) to preserve visual boundary clarity against background gradient blobs.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through optical translucency, refractive glassmorphism, and color-cast luminescence rather than traditional opaque shadows.

### The Glass Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas (Level 0)**: `#0B0B0F` solid background with blurred neon SVG/radial gradient blobs positioned at points of discovery (e.g., `#7C5CFF` behind AI modules, `#FF3D7F` behind hot recommendations). Blur diameter: 80px–120px.
- **Frosted Panels (Level 1 - Feed Cards, Menus)**: `rgba(22, 22, 29, 0.75)` backdrop fill, 12px to 16px background blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), combined with a crisp perimeter highlight: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)`.
- **Raised Interactive (Level 2 - Active Modals, Flyouts, Docked Nav)**: `rgba(30, 30, 40, 0.85)`, 24px background blur, perimeter highlight: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)`, ambient downward drop: `0 16px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.

### Chromatic Glows
Focus, selection, and engagement activate ambient color glows:
- Primary interactive glow: `0 0 20px rgba(255, 61, 127, 0.35)`.
- AI suggestion highlight: `0 0 24px rgba(124, 92, 255, 0.40)`.
- Success / Live indicator glow: `0 0 16px rgba(198, 255, 61, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The design uses an ultra-soft, organic pill-inspired shape language that balances tactile playfulness with ergonomic handheld gestures.

- **Buttons & Chips**: Fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full`, 9999px) for single-line interactions, food filter tags, and quick-prompts.
- **Cards & Sheets**: Large, squircle-like radiuses ranging from 20px (`rounded-lg` token scale) to 28px (`rounded-xl` token scale) for primary media tiles, AI conversation cards, and modal bottom drawers.
- **Thumbnails & Badges**: 12px to 14px rounding for embedded food photography thumbnails within nested lists.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Crave Now / Book)**: Full pill (`rounded-full`), solid `#FF3D7F` fill, pure white text (`#FFFFFF`, Space Grotesk 14px/700). On hover/active: scale `0.97` with dynamic glow `0 0 24px rgba(255, 61, 127, 0.5)`.
- **Secondary (AI Assist / Explore)**: Full pill, transparent glass interior (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`), border `1px solid rgba(124, 92, 255, 0.6)`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary**: Full pill, transparent, hover background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Default Filter**: Pill shape, `rgba(22, 22, 29, 0.75)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text `#B0B0C4`. Height 36px, horizontal padding `1rem`.
- **Active Filter**: Border color transitions to `#C6FF3D`, background shifts to `rgba(198, 255, 61, 0.12)`, text `#C6FF3D` with subtle glow.

### Cards (Restaurant & AI Recommendation)
- Built with `rgba(22, 22, 29, 0.75)`, `backdrop-blur-md`, 24px corner radius, and `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)`.
- Hero food imagery is masked with a 16px internal radius, overlaid with gradient vignettes fading to card background.
- Floating metric badges (e.g., rating `4.9 ★`, "Indiranagar") reside in frosted sub-pills (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`, blur 8px).

### Input Fields & Conversational AI Bar
- **AI Prompt Field**: Docked or floating bottom bar, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), height 56px, filled with `rgba(26, 26, 36, 0.85)` and framed by an electric gradient border (`linear-gradient(90deg, #FF3D7F, #7C5CFF)`).
- **Placeholder Text**: `#76768E`, shifting to `#FFFFFF` on typing.
- **Focus State**: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px #7C5CFF, 0 0 20px rgba(124, 92, 255, 0.35)`.

### Checkboxes, Radio Buttons, & Toggles
- **Toggles**: Pill track (`48px x 28px`), inactive state `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`, active state `#C6FF3D`. Thumb is solid white circle (`22px`), translating smoothly with spring physics.
- **Radio Buttons / Checkboxes**: 20px circular units with 1.5px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`. Active state fills with `#FF3D7F` containing a white check icon or center pip.

### Lists & Menu Items
- Separated not by solid opaque dividers, but by hairline gradients `linear-gradient(90deg, transparent, rgba(255,255,255,0.08), transparent)`.
- Interactive list tiles exhibit hover state brightening via `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` fill and smooth horizontal translation (+4px).

### Specialized Domain Components
- **Vibe Meter**: Frosted horizontal progress pill indicating ambient venue noise/crowd levels using a gradient ramp from `#C6FF3D` (Chill) to `#FF3D7F` (Electric).
- **AI Crave Badge**: Pill featuring a pulsing micro-dot (`#7C5CFF`) indicating dynamic algorithmic match affinity (e.g., "98% Match for your late-night mood").
